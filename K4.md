
map, so it is internally consistent with the fitting target. The original grid
remains untouched in `capture.pt`.

**Unit caveat:** `1/metre` means inverse depth in the captured SHARP metric-coordinate

physical distances to the bridge. TinyViM raw relative disparity is never inverted.

### Alignment implementation

New isolated module: `experiments/sharp_hybrid_research/alignment.py`.

1. Exclude nonfinite pixels, invalid target depth, padded regions, cached known-wire
   proxies, strong RGB and robust-spread-normalized SHARP boundaries, three-pixel
   boundary shoulders and16px tile borders. No blue-sky or raw-depth heuristic.
2. Identify disconnected **fitting interiors**, not automatic replacement/surface
   ownership. Regions smaller than64 pixels are excluded.
3. Form sigma24/3-sigma-truncated common-bandwidth fitting maps by normalized
   Gaussian filtering separately inside each interior. Invalid/boundary samples
   cannot enter numerators; different region IDs do not mix. Exclude kernel mass
   below0.05. Record support radius72 native pixels. Sigma0 is a predeclared control.
4. Stratify by32px spatial block,4 target quantile bins and interior ID. Cap each
   bucket at64 samples. Each `(interior, quantile)` pair receives equal total mass;
   its blocks receive mass proportional to their **capped** sample count. Sparse
   buckets are not amplified to the mass of full buckets.
5. Fit `q=a*r+b` with float64 weighted least squares, median/P90-P10 conditioning,
  15 Huber IRLS iterations and optional top5% trimming. No absolute slope guard.
6. Hold out whole spatial blocks across3 folds. Critically, sigma24 fitting maps
   are **recomputed separately from training-only and validation-only raw samples**
   in every fold. A training smoothing kernel never reads held-out depth values.
7. Accept only a positive full fit and positive identifiable folds with normalized
   holdout median<0.1, p90<0.25, positive-fold scale variation<20%. Require128 sampled
   anchors and4 blocks. Anchor P90-P10 must cover at least2% of the corresponding
   full-map P90-P10 range; numerical degeneracy uses floating-point-relative checks.
8. Apply an accepted/attempted positive affine transform to the **original sharp
   raw TinyViM array**, not the smoothed fitting map. Rejected fits never expose a
   usable transformed-pixel mask. A negative slope is recorded as a diagnostic,
   never clipped, flipped or accepted.

These are fixed development gates, not universal truth. They were not relaxed per
crop. Small coefficient magnitude is not considered a failure.

### Two implementation issues discovered and corrected

* Initial synthetic outlier recovery failed because equal weighting of every
  spatial bucket boosted sparse outlier-only buckets into a weighted majority.
  The capped-count weighting above fixed this while preserving bucket caps and
  balancing. The outlier test now recovers the affine coefficients and correctly
  rejects poor p90 validation when held-out outliers remain.
* The first real diagnostic pass used whole-block holdout on globally pre-smoothed
  maps. Although anchor indices were disjoint, their kernels shared data. That run
  remains under `results/sharp_hybrid/k4_alignment/` for traceability and is **not
  authoritative validation**. The corrected run is
  `results/sharp_hybrid/k4_alignment_crossfit/`. A focused test verifies disjoint
  training/validation kernel source masks. No neural inference was repeated for
  this correction; the same cached arrays were reused.

## Remaining boundary

K3 now has real-checkpoint evidence and a reusable real baseline. K4 has an executable
alignment implementation, numerical tests, authoritative diagnostics and a negative/
underdetermined real-data finding. It does not supply an accepted wire-distance
calibration for these crops.

Before using the new detail as geometry, independently verify common foreground/
background anchors or another trustworthy context/component. The present fitting
interiors are conservative and often remove textured foreground; carefully checked
correspondences may distinguish an anchor-selection failure from genuine
non-affine/global-context disagreement. That follow-up must keep spatial validation
and report failures rather than relaxing gates until a crop passes.

K5 would decide replacement/ownership and evaluate substitution/gradient-domain
fusion. K6 would test native→1536→768 survival, composed Gaussian behavior and nearby
views. Neither stage was implemented here. There is no claim of successful fusion,
improved hybrid Gaussians, halo-free rendering or improved nearby-view geometry.
